Plate II — The daily reading
02 / 09

One screen answers
“why do I feel
this way today?”

01 · CHECK-IN

One tap, no form. Mood is logged with an abstract shape, not a smiley — no judgement attached.

02 · CHART

Nine days at a glance. Today sits in the centre, so the eye reads trend, not a single number.

03 · CYCLES

Three rings, three systems. Body, emotion and mind are separated — a dip in one isn’t a bad day.

04 · ACTION

Logging is always one thumb away. The centre button stays reachable in one-handed use.
