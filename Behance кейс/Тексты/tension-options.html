C · Three phones on an arc. The screens float side by side on a soft arc (centre one higher), tilted outward like objects on an orbit. Below, the three tensions as quiet columns. Compact and gallery‑like.
D · Problem above, decision below. A thin horizon line splits the block: the three problems sit above it, dim and grey; the decisions below it, bright. The phones float across the line, half in each zone, as the bridge between them.
E · Fixed headline, cascading pairs. The headline stays large in the left column; on the right the pairs cascade down, each phone overlapping the next a little, like cards laid on a dark table.
F · Triptych rising from below. Three tall columns, each with its tension at the top and a phone rising from the bottom edge, cropped, exactly like the phone on the cover. Ties the block to the cover and keeps it very airy.
