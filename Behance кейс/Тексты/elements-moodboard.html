M1 · Three depths on a diagonal. Near images large and at full brightness, middle ones smaller and dimmed, far ones tiny, dim and a little soft. They drift along one diagonal from top left to bottom right, so the chaos has a direction.
M2 · On an orbit. The images sit on one invisible tilted ellipse, like objects circling a planet: in front large and bright, behind small and fading. The case’s orbit motif again.
M3 · The astrolabe at the centre. The astrolabe large and bright in the middle; the other images around it in two loose rings, dimmer and smaller the further out they are.
M4 · A drifting grid. A hidden six-column grid holds everything, but each image floats at its own height and depth inside its column; every other one recedes. Calm, but alive.
M5 · A band of light. All images gathered in one horizontal band through the middle, overlapping; the centre is bright and close, both ends sink into the dark.
