Reflection · five more layouts

R5 · Three sentences. Three large, quiet statements stacked with lots of air, each with one small line under it. Reads like conclusions, not a list.
R6 · I thought / I learned. Three pairs side by side: what I assumed at the start, struck through softly, and what the project taught me instead.
R7 · The process line, continued. The Glance line Discover · Design · Build gets a fourth station, Next, glowing; under each station what it gave me, under Next the honest next steps.

R9 · Done and next. Two calm halves: what is done (the science path, the kit, the app, the audit) and what is next, as two short lists with a thin divider.

Closing · five ways to say thank you

K1 · The app’s logo. The real Soma logo from the kit, the glowing orbital ring with “Soma” inside, on the star field; one small line under it.
K2 · Mirror of the cover. The cover’s top line returns at the bottom (project · role · year); the motto small above a large, light “Thank you”.
K3 · Practice complete. The real “Well done! You finished the practice” screen as the last image; next to it “Thank you for watching”. The case ends like a calm practice does.
K4 · Ring and words. The logo’s ring alone, large and faint, with “Thank you for watching” written inside it instead of the name.
K5 · Just stars. Almost empty: the star field, a tiny logo ring and two words. The quietest ending.
