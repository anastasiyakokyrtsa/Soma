1 · Beams of light. Each colour is a tall slim beam that glows at the bottom and dissolves upwards into the dark, like light rising from a horizon. Name and hex below each beam.
2 · Mostly silence, a few signals. One long bar split by how much of the screen each colour takes: the night background fills most of it, violet a little, the bright signals only a sliver. Shows the hierarchy of the palette, not just the list.
3 · Light trails. Each colour drawn as one long thin glowing line, gently curved like a star trail in a long exposure, stacked from the deepest at the bottom to the brightest on top. Labels at the end of each trail.
4 · Words in their colour. No swatches at all: the token names written large in their own colour, flowing like a line of text, hex in small next to each. Fits the type-led look of the block.
5 · Layers of atmosphere. The palette as soft horizontal layers of one sky, from the deep night at the top to a faint glow at the horizon, blending into each other. Thin labels on the right edge of each layer.
6 · Glass panels lit from inside. Tall glass panels in the app’s card style, each glowing softly from below in one colour, like a screen in the dark. Name and hex engraved at the top.
