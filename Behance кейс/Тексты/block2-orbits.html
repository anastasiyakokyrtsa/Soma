C1 · Orbit with meaning · the original C, plus one quiet line at each step. The ring fades along its length like a comet tail, so it reads as one direction of motion: observe → understand → respond → back to observe
C2 · Tilted orbit · the loop seen in perspective, like a planet’s orbit around a quiet centre (“you”). The far side of the orbit is dimmer, the near node is larger. Uses the full width under the text, very “inner cosmos”
C3 · Three orbits · each step on its own ring around one centre, like the three cycles the app tracks (23 / 28 / 33 days). Shows that the steps are separate rhythms that keep turning together
C4 · Horizon · only the top arc of a huge orbit is visible, like the curve of a planet at the bottom of the frame. Three steps sit on the arc. Calm, cinematic, and it leads the eye into the next block
C5 · Orbit of particles · the ring is made of the same luminous dust as the onboarding illustrations, denser near the three steps. The most direct link to the app’s visual language
C6 · Endless rhythm · a thin figure‑eight loop: observe on one side, respond on the other, understand where the line crosses itself. Says “this never really ends, it’s a rhythm”, without drawing a single arrow
