Block 4 · The tension. Two failure modes from the CJM, set against each other. In the middle, the design response, which leads straight into the “two ways in” idea.
03 / The tension

Too clinical to trust.
Too esoteric to believe.

Too clinical
Information‑heavy apps push people away when they are already anxious.
Long forms before any first result.
No energy left to act, even on simple advice.
Too esoteric
Fear that it is one more superficial app.
Doubt whether the conclusions can be trusted.
Beauty that feels like it says nothing about you.
Design response
One calm observatory, spoken in the language you trust.
